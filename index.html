Flagship Project

Karonga Basin Bamboo
Carbon Credit Project — Malawi

Commissioned by the Small Scale Livestock and Livelihoods Programme (SSLLP) — an established Malawi NGO — Agriforests designed a complete VERRA AFOLU carbon project targeting 37,000 smallholder farmers across the Karonga District of northern Malawi. Built entirely pro bono over three years with full intellectual property retained by Agriforests.

The project applies VM0047 methodology with Dendrocalamus asper (highland) and Bambusa vulgaris (lowland/drought-tolerant) — selected from INBAR's global species database — projecting 75 tCO₂e/ha/year at a 20% buffer zone deduction. Land eligibility was established using Global Forest Watch and SOTERSAF soil mapping to document over 10 years of uninterrupted agricultural land use with no evidence of recent deforestation.

Governance legitimacy was secured through a prolonged engagement process resulting in dual Letters of Endorsement from Chief Paramount Kyungu of the Karonga District Traditional Authority and the Karonga District Council — a rare and significant achievement in AFOLU project development.
